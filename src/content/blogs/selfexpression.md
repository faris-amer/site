# self expression & the void

It's hard to express frustration with the current online social sphere without coming off as pretentious, and to be honest, there's more than enough negativity and doomerism online as it is. 


This website is my attempt at adressing my own frustration. my corner of the internet, which i painstakingly carved out of $7/year for two reasons:
number one -  a better portfolio (hire me I work hard I promise) and number two - a place for me to share my thoughts, like this page you're scrolling through right now.

I don't like the social media page format and how it encourages validation-seeking behaviour, with likes on your posts and stories and comments and your messages with other people. The instant reaction and optimization and the infinite content discourages a lot of really good social behviour that I want to sidestep. but enough of 'social media bad'

I'm very inspired by people who do express themselves in any means, especially if it's into the void. It's easy to appreciate famous artists and musicians and creatives but the my favourites are the people who do it for the love of the game. like the people of [neocities](https://neocities.org/browse) or the early youtube era, people who paint or draw or sculpt and set their creations on shelves and walls for people to see. And really, everyone is, whether or not you realize it.

Self expression is important in cementing your place in this world. What really makes you different than the next guy over? Sure you've got a different name and you look a little different but inside, you're a totally different person. And it's a miracle just how different people are, but it can be hard to get across what makes you... you, because self expression means being vulnerable. Sharing a piece of yourself is opening yourself up for criticism, and if you aren't ready for that kind of heat, it can be pretty daunting. But it doesn't have to be!

Which is why this medium is my favourite. This website exists in the void - I have no idea if you're reading this, and no idea if you like it or not. The only feedback i'll ever get is a direct message or email about it, which shows care and effort past a comment on a social media page.

So please, show more of yourself to the world. Not in posting a hundred times a day, but in a single post that only you could have created. Share your poetry and paragraph long rants about the things that piss you off, and the improv comedy bits you come up with in the shower - it's what social media was made for. 

I am painfully aware of my naive worldview, and I'll likely look back on many of the things I say in a few months or years and disgaree with a lot of the takes I had. But the only way to grow is to practice, and if it means writing a few cliche paragraphs a month, so be it. Writing and communication is a skill I care a lot about perserving for myself, and I don't get enough opportunities to write much of anything in my day-to-day, so this is my little sandbox to do with as I please. [This video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WIP_hLaLnLo) was a big inspiration that gave me the push to actually finish and publish things I write.

So that's what I'll be doing here. I'll try to post something every few months, sometimes more, sometimes less. I also have a lot of ideas for the website that are coming soon (whenever I have the motivation).

thanks for reading
